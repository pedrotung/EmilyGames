Colour Jump

Your block keeps moving forward.
Tap JUMP to clear the gaps and blocks.
Walk into each coloured door to reach the next of 7 levels.

Tap JUMP to start. On a computer, press Space.

Paused

Tap JUMP or Resume to keep going.

Full screen

This browser can't hide its toolbars for a web page. To play full screen, add the game to your home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.

Then open Colour Jump from the new icon.
